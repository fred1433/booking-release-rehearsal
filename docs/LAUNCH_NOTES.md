# What a booking launch has to answer, seen from the public preview

Source: a public "launching soon" preview page of an online marketplace for private dance sessions, read on 14 October 2026. Nothing here comes from that company's code, systems or data, which we have not seen. The page itself says its sessions are examples and that bookings open at launch.

## Visible on the page

- Sessions of 30, 45 or 60 minutes.
- Prices shown in euros, US dollars and Australian dollars, depending on the example.
- Example teachers based in several countries and time zones.
- "Book instantly or request a time".
- Comparing specialists, prices and reviews.
- A certification badge on some sessions.
- Browsing by focus, experience level, language and availability.
- Some example sessions are aimed at young dancers.

## Questions these raise for launch (deductions, not findings)

- **Overlapping lengths.** With three session lengths, a teacher can be double-booked by sessions that start at different times. The guarantee has to sit where bookings are written. This rehearsal tests exactly that.
- **Time zones and clock changes.** Teacher and student often live in different zones, and their clock changes fall on different dates (Sydney moved forward on 4 October 2026, Paris moves back on 25 October 2026). Store the instant, show each person their own local time, and decide what happens to recurring availability around the change.
- **Currency.** Which currency is actually charged, which is only displayed, and who carries the exchange difference.
- **Request a time.** A second state machine: pending, accepted, declined, expired, cancelled. Does a pending request hold the slot? What if an instant booking takes it first?
- **Payment and payout.** Payment failures, retries, refunds and paying teachers in other countries (a deduction from international teachers; nothing on the page says how payouts work).
- **After the booking.** Confirmation, the session link, reminders across time zones, cancellation and no-show rules, support.
- **Trust signals.** Who can leave a review (only after a completed session?) and where the certification badge comes from.
- **Young learners.** Whether a parent or guardian books and is contacted.

This rehearsal covers only the first two, for instant booking, with simulated payment.
